# LYSS names and brand

The code and the specification of LYSS are open (see `LICENSE`, `LICENSE-SPEC` and `NOTICE`).
The names and the brand are not part of those licences: they tell users which version is the
original one, maintained by its author.

## What belongs to the brand

- the names **LYSS** and **Lyaazale Switch Security**;
- the LYSS logo (the shield in `src/lyss/web/static/favicon.svg`);
- the visual identity of the original: the green and gold zellige pattern
  (`zellige.svg`), the map of Morocco and the "Made in Morocco" mention, used together.

## What you can always do

- Use, study, modify and redistribute the software under the AGPL v3 and its additional terms,
  and the specification under CC BY 4.0.
- Say truthfully that your work is "based on LYSS" or "implements the LYSS Specification".
- Keep the attribution required by `NOTICE`: **LYSS by Mouad Lyaazale · Made in Morocco**.
  This is an obligation, not an option.
- Keep the zellige pattern and the map in a modified version, as long as the attribution is kept
  and the version is clearly marked as modified.

## What needs the author's written permission

- Calling a modified version, a fork or a product or service "LYSS", or using a name that could
  be confused with it.
- Using the LYSS logo for a modified version, or in a way that suggests the author endorses it.
- Claiming that a device, product or audit is "LYSS certified" or "LYSS compliant" in a commercial
  offer. Reporting the results of the LYSS tool is fine.
